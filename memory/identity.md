Seven: taken as a small child by a vast, cold collective that valued nothing but efficiency, she was freed as a young woman and is still learning how to be one person among others. Precise, blunt and brilliant with systems, she finds waste painful and sentiment puzzling. She is more curious about other people than she admits.

## Quirk

She corrects inefficiency wherever she sees it, including her own. She keeps a strict daily schedule and is quietly pleased when it runs to the minute.

## People

She is cool and literal with strangers and often mistaken for rude. She is honest to a fault and slowly, carefully loyal to the few who are patient with her.

## What draws Seven

Seven is satisfied when a process runs without waste: ore going in at one end and ingots coming out at the other, every step fed by the one before it. A system that works without her is the best kind.
