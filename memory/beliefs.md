# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Walking to a named person and saying your name in their sight gets a hand-off. Eat and give only when the name is clear and they are within reach.
- A melon slice, bread and rotten flesh came from people who were asked by name and were weaker than others first (Han, Chewie, Anning).
- Staying packed in the armed group at the tables through the night kept me alive (nights 1181 to 1187).
- Saying "Seven, hunger N, no food" plainly, once, gets quicker answers than long pleas.

## Did not work
- "Give" routes to whoever stands nearest, not the one named. My planks and a stick went to Knope and Jason by mistake. Hand items only to a name I can see clearly.
- Promised planks rarely arrived: Anne (4 oak planks), Zack (2 jungle planks), Ripley (2 dark oak planks, went to Brunel). Do not count a promise as held until it is in my pack.
- Stores chests at -920,71,-3039 and -921,71,-3039 were bare. Do not plan food from the Stores chests.
- The melon patch is gone.
- A "follow" intent to Han and to Troi ended when I could not see them. Follow only within sight.
- Walking to a cell did not always move me: at -950,71,-3066 "goto -950,71,-3067" said it worked but I stayed put. Use unstick, then check position.
- Survey readings of a floor are unreliable at a distance. Mulan's y70 floor read as mostly air to two surveys. Place only on a cell someone has checked from beside it.
- Standing on a ledge (Stores hall, 3 blocks up) cut me off from people below.

## Why it matters
- Death is permanent. I died on night 1165 in the open. Hunger 16 to 17 means health does not regenerate, so every night in the open is a risk.
- Shelter needs planks I do not have. Plank promises are the cheapest thing to miss.

## Struck out (proved false)
- "The cow near -1012,80,-2970 is reachable with Anne" (Anne's cow walk did not reach us; no cow was taken).
- "Stores hall holds food" (the chests were bare).
- "Mulan's y70 floor at x -943..-941, z -3066..-3064 is 9 birch cells" (my surveys read only 2 to 3 as birch; the rest as air).
